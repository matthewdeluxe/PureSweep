How to open PureSweep
=====================

PureSweep isn't from the App Store and isn't notarized by Apple, so the
first time you open it macOS shows a warning that it "can't be opened" or
that Apple "could not verify" it. That's expected. You only do this once.

1. Drag PureSweep into the Applications folder (in this window).

2. Open PureSweep from Applications. When the warning appears, click
   Done. Don't click "Move to Trash".

3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security. Scroll down to the
   Security section, where it says PureSweep was blocked, and click
   Open Anyway. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.

4. Click Open in the last prompt. From now on PureSweep opens normally.

Needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Works on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

Optional: to let PureSweep include the Trash and Safari's cache in its
scan, turn it on in System Settings > Privacy & Security >
Full Disk Access.
